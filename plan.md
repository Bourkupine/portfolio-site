# ☕ Project Plan: Euan's Coffee & Zen Portfolio Website

Welcome to your portfolio development plan! This project will be built with a highly custom, ultra-premium, responsive design blending the warmth of **Coffee Culture** with the refined minimalist lines of **Japanese Design Aesthetics**.

The site will be deployed directly via GitHub Pages on [www.euan.ie](http://www.euan.ie).

---

## 🎨 Theme & Visual Identity

We are blending two gorgeous design concepts:
1. **Coffee Palette (The Roast Levels)**:
   - **Dark Mode (Americano / Espresso)**: Deep roasted coffee-bean browns (`#160F0C`, `#261B16`) paired with silky cream text (`#ECE5D8`).
   - **Light Mode (Latte / Cappuccino)**: Soft, warm creamy whites (`#FDFBF7`, `#F7F3EB`) paired with rich dark espresso text (`#2D1E18`).
2. **Japanese Accents (Zen Highlights)**:
   - **Urushi Lacquer Red / Sakura (`#C94A49` / `#E05D5C`)**: A striking, deep vermillion red for call-to-actions, active states, and custom underlines.
   - **Matcha Green (`#5D7A57` / `#9BBF97`)**: A calm, earthy green for secondary badges, successful interactions, and tags.
3. **Layout & Feel**:
   - **Shoji-Screen Structure**: Minimalist, clean grids, fine dividers, and spacious layouts reminiscent of traditional Japanese architecture.
   - **Frosted Glass (Milk Foam)**: High-end glassmorphic cards with soft blur effects and warm, subtle borders.
   - **Micro-Animations**: Smooth scroll effects, steam rising from coffee illustrations, and subtle tactile hover feedback.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture & Tech Stack

- **Core**: Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 (Modern custom variables, Flexbox/Grid, transitions), and Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Performance**: High performance, zero external framework dependencies, ultra-fast loading speed, 100% SEO optimized.
- **Responsiveness**: Mobile-first fluid design (works beautifully from small screens to ultra-wide monitors).
- **Deployment**: Configured to run flawlessly on GitHub Pages using `CNAME`.

## ☕ Site Structure ("The Menu")

### 1. 📋 Header & Navigation ("The Order Station")
- Floating café-menu-style navigation bar that docks elegantly at the top or bottom of the screen.
- A **"Brew Your Theme" selector** representing different drinks:
  - ☕ **Americano** (Deep Dark Mode)
  - 🥛 **Latte** (Creamy Light Mode)
  - 🍵 **Matcha** (Earthy Green Accent Mode)

### 2. ⚡ Hero Section ("The Freshly Brewed Code")
- A bold, punchy headline introducing **Euan** as a Software Engineer & Web Developer.
- A beautiful, CSS-animated minimal coffee cup graphic with subtle rising steam.
- A brief tagline: *"Crafting high-performance, robust code with the care of an artisan barista."*
- Dynamic call-to-action buttons ("View the Menu" and "Get in Touch").

### 3. ☕ The Beans & Roast ("About Me")
- A clean, personal narrative section sharing your journey, engineering philosophy, and interests.
- A split layout: one side for your bio (focused on craftsmanship, precision, and simplicity), the other side highlighting your favorite tools and workspace.

### 4. 🏷️ Selected Blends ("Projects Showcase")
- A collection of your best work presented as curated "Brews", complete with **"Roast Indicators"**:
  - 🟡 **Light Roast**: High-fidelity frontend, interactive UI, and polished designs.
  - 🟠 **Medium Roast**: Full-stack web apps, robust backend APIs, and integrations.
  - 🟤 **Dark Roast**: High-performance systems, low-level architecture, or complex data processing.
- Interactive cards with custom hover expansions, project descriptions, list of ingredients (technologies used), and clean links to GitHub and live sites.

### 5. 🌿 Flavour Profiles ("Skills & Ingredients")
- A grid of your core technical competencies (Frontend, Backend, Tools & DevOps, Methodologies).
- Interactive cards featuring elegant icons, glowing badges, and precise category labels.

### 6. ✍️ Brewing Notes / Journal ("Blog")
- A clean, elegant, minimal writing section showing recent technical and non-technical articles.
- Article cards styled like craft coffee bags, featuring read-time tags, publication dates, summaries, and smooth expansions.

### 7. 📬 Grab a Cup ("Contact")
- An ultra-sleek, interactive contact form designed to look like a **coffee order ticket/receipt**.
- Social media and developer platform links styled as minimalist stamps.
